# Read Aloud

Create a short passage for candidates to read aloud. Should have no audio or image requirements.

## Requirements
- Passage: 50-60 words (approximately 15-20 seconds at normal speech)
- No numbers or abbreviations
- Clear, natural English
- Suitable for fluency assessment
- No special characters or complex punctuation
